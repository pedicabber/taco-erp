# PRD #18 – Safety Module (LOTO Management System)

## Priority

High Priority

---

# Objective

Create a Safety module within the ERP that provides a fully digital Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) workflow.

The system will eliminate paper checklists, establish accountability through a designated LOTO Commander, create a permanent audit trail, and provide real-time visibility into active lockouts across projects, installations, FATs, service work, and shop operations.

# Checklist Sections

## Preparation

* Energy sources identified
* Shutdown procedure reviewed
* Personnel notified
* Locks/tags available

---

## Shutdown

* Equipment shut down
* Equipment fully stopped

---

## Isolation

* Electrical
* Pneumatic
* Hydraulic
* Mechanical
* Gravity
* Other

---

## Lock Application

* Lock applied
* Tag applied
* Name entered
* Date entered
* Reason entered

---

## Stored Energy

* Pressure relieved
* Capacitors discharged
* Moving parts secured
* Residual energy removed

---

## Verify Zero Energy

* Startup attempted
* No movement
* Energy absent
* Controls returned OFF

Completion changes status to ACTIVE.

---

# Work Phase

Allow:

* Notes
* Photos
* Personnel additions
* Issue documentation

---

# Request Release

Technician completes:

* Work complete
* Tools removed
* Guards installed
* Area cleaned
* Personnel clear

Status becomes:

Pending Commander Review

---

# Commander Review

LOTO Commander verifies:

* Work complete
* Guards installed
* Personnel clear
* Area safe
* Safe to energize

Commander may add comments.

---

# Authorize Energization

LOTO Commander authorizes power restoration.

System records:

* User
* Date
* Time
* Comments

---

# Closeout

Status changes to CLOSED.

Record automatically moves into LOTO History.

---

# LOTO History

Store permanently:

* Project
* Equipment
* Commander
* Locked Out By
* Start Time
* End Time
* Notes
* Photos
* Authorization Records

---

# Acceptance Criteria

* Users can create a LOTO from desktop or mobile.
* Active LOTO records are visible company-wide.
* Commander approval is required before closure.
* Full audit trail is retained.
* Closed records are searchable.
* No paper forms required.
