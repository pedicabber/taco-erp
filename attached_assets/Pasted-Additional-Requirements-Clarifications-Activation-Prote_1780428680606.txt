Additional Requirements & Clarifications

### Activation Protection

A LOTO record shall not transition to ACTIVE status unless all required checklist items in the following sections are completed:

* Preparation
* Shutdown
* Isolation
* Lock Application
* Stored Energy
* Verify Zero Energy

Partial completion shall not permit activation.

---

### Project Visibility

Because every LOTO record is tied to a Project, active lockouts shall be visible directly from the associated Project page.

If a Project contains one or more active lockouts, display a prominent warning banner.

Example:

⚠ ACTIVE LOTO EXISTS

LOTO #25
Commander: Justin Ferris
Status: ACTIVE

Selecting the banner shall navigate directly to the LOTO record.

---

### Draft Visibility

The Safety Dashboard shall include:

🟡 Draft LOTO Count

This allows incomplete or abandoned lockout records to be identified and managed.

Dashboard Summary Cards:

* Draft LOTO
* Active LOTO
* Pending Commander Review
* Closed This Month
* Critical Lockouts Active

---

### Commander Notification

When a technician submits Request Release and a LOTO transitions to Pending Commander Review:

* The assigned LOTO Commander shall receive an in-app notification.
* The notification shall contain:

  * LOTO Number
  * Project
  * Equipment
  * Requesting User

Selecting the notification shall open the LOTO record.

---

### Attachment Restrictions

Supported attachment types:

* Images / Photos
* PDF Documents
* Common Office Documents

Maximum upload size shall follow the existing attachment standards used elsewhere in the ERP.

Unsupported file types shall be rejected.

---

### Historical Record Protection

Closed LOTO records shall be immutable.

Users may:

* View
* Search
* Filter
* Export

Users may not modify:

* Checklist responses
* Commander approvals
* Authorization timestamps
* Attachments
* Audit history

after closure.

Any future corrections shall require creation of a new administrative audit record rather than modification of the original LOTO.

---

### Future Safety Expansion

The Safety module architecture shall allow future addition of:

* Safety Audits
* Incident Reports
* Training Records

without requiring redesign of the existing LOTO system.
